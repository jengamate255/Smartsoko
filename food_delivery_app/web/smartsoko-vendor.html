Our Story

Founded in 2016, Kilimanjaro Fresh started as a small family farm on the fertile slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro. Today, we work with over 50 local farmers to bring the freshest organic produce to your table.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, organic, and sustainably grown produce while supporting local farming communities and promoting healthy eating habits across Tanzania.
