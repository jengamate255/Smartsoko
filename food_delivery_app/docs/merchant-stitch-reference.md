The reference uses warm paper, forest green, clay waiting indicators, editorial revenue typography, compact metrics, and receipt-style order details. It is a sample-data design concept. Payment verification, VAT, ticket printing, and other synthetic content in the reference are not production capabilities or evidence of payment.

Implemented in the application: mobile Store pulse disclosure keeps revenue visible, hides secondary metrics initially, supports aria-expanded and keyboard focus, and restores all metrics at desktop widths. No live customer data was sent to Stitch.

Suggested future concepts from Stitch: an optional kitchen alert sound, an expanded ticket filter, and a kitchen display view. No additional generation was requested.
