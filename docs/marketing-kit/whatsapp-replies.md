# SmartSoko enquiry replies

21 October 2026 · Copy-and-paste templates for replies to incoming enquiries. Nothing has been sent or configured in WhatsApp.

## Greeting / Salamu

Habari! Karibu SmartSoko. Tunajiandaa kuanza majaribio Kunduchi na Tegeta, na tunapanga kupanua huduma Dar es Salaam. Ungependa kupata taarifa kama mteja, au kuulizia kujiunga kama muuzaji?

Hello! Welcome to SmartSoko. We are preparing a pilot in Kunduchi and Tegeta, with expansion planned across Dar es Salaam. Are you interested in customer updates or joining as a merchant?

## Customer interest / Mteja anayetaka taarifa

Asante kwa kutuandikia! Uko Kunduchi, Tegeta au eneo jingine? Ungependa kuagiza bidhaa gani? Ukipenda, tunaweza kukutumia taarifa hapa huduma itakapoanza. Je, unakubali kupata taarifa hizo? Unaweza kutuambia tusitishe wakati wowote.

Thanks for getting in touch! Are you in Kunduchi, Tegeta or another area? What would you like to order? With your permission, we can message you here when the service starts. Would you like those updates? You can ask us to stop at any time.

## After they agree / Baada ya kukubali

Asante! Tutakutumia taarifa za uzinduzi kupitia mazungumzo haya. Tarehe ya kuanza bado haijathibitishwa.

Thank you! We will send launch updates in this chat. The launch date has not yet been confirmed.

Use this only after recording their agreement. Do not claim that a registration has been saved in an app unless you actually saved it.

## Outside the pilot / Nje ya eneo la majaribio

Kwa sasa tunajiandaa kuanza Kunduchi na Tegeta. Tunapanga kupanua huduma Dar es Salaam, lakini hatujathibitisha tarehe ya kufika eneo lako. Ukikubali, tunaweza kukutumia taarifa huduma itakapopatikana karibu nawe.

We are preparing to start in Kunduchi and Tegeta. We plan to expand across Dar es Salaam, but have not confirmed when your area will be covered. With your permission, we can update you when service becomes available nearby.

## Merchant enquiry / Muuzaji anayeulizia kujiunga

Asante kwa nia ya kujiunga na SmartSoko! Tafadhali tuambie jina la biashara, eneo, aina ya bidhaa na saa za kazi. Tunaweza kupanga mazungumzo mafupi kuhusu namna ya kuweka bidhaa, kupokea oda, usafirishaji, gharama na malipo. Masharti yatajadiliwa kabla ya kujiunga.

Thank you for your interest in joining SmartSoko! Please share your business name, area, product categories and opening hours. We can arrange a short discussion about listing products, orders, delivery, fees and settlement. Terms will be discussed before you join.

## Launch date, price and delivery questions

Tarehe ya kuanza, gharama na utaratibu wa usafirishaji vitathibitishwa kabla ya uzinduzi. Tutakueleza taarifa hizo zitakapokuwa tayari; hatutaki kukuahidi jambo ambalo halijathibitishwa.

The launch date, charges and delivery arrangements will be confirmed before launch. We will share those details once they are ready.

## Someone tries to place an order before launch

Asante kwa nia ya kuagiza. Bado hatujaanza kupokea oda kupitia majaribio haya. Ukikubali, tutakutumia taarifa ya kuanza na njia ya kuagiza itakapokuwa tayari.

Thank you for your interest in ordering. We are not yet taking orders through this pilot. With your permission, we can send the launch update and ordering instructions when they are ready.

## Stop request / Ombi la kusitisha taarifa

Tumepokea ombi lako. Hatutakutumia tena taarifa za kampeni hii. Asante kwa kutujulisha.

We have received your request and will stop sending you updates for this campaign. Thank you for letting us know.

Record the stop request before reusing any campaign list.
