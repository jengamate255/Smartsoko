## Suggested first-week posting order

1. Introduce SmartSoko with the customer flyer.
2. Publish merchant recruitment with the merchant flyer.
3. Ask customers what they would order.
4. Share a short real product walkthrough with test data clearly identified.
5. Share a participating merchant story only with their agreement and confirmed participation.
6. Answer actual questions about the pilot; do not invent fees or dates.
7. Repeat the registration CTA and record customer interest and merchant enquiries separately.

Measure enquiries, completed interest registrations, qualified merchant conversations and confirmed merchant participation. Likes and views are attention measures, not orders or revenue.
