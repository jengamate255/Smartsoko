## Two-minute pitch

SmartSoko is a local marketplace for food and everyday essentials. Our starting hypothesis is simple: customers want an easier way to order from nearby merchants, while those merchants need a manageable way to accept and fulfil online orders.

We are building the customer storefront together with the merchant order desk and driver workflow, so an order can move through one coordinated process. The repository includes these product components, and we still need to complete end-to-end operating validation before making reliability claims.

## Answers to likely questions

**Why only TZS 5 million?** It funds a bounded pilot, not a nationwide launch. We will confirm scope against supplier quotes and the founder's available time before committing to a duration.

**What is already proven?** Product implementation is visible. Commercial performance must be supported by dated order and payment records; we have not verified those figures for this draft.

**What makes this defensible?** The hypothesis is that merchant relationships, fulfilment density and customer retention can create an advantage over time. We are not claiming a proven moat today.

**Are payments and payouts automated?** There is payment and reconciliation implementation. Recent documentation describes manual external merchant transfers with recording inside the platform. Complete staging and live validation before claiming full automation or reliability.

**What equity is offered?** Terms are not set. First agree the pilot scope, funding structure and reporting expectations; do not promise ownership percentages or returns from this draft.
