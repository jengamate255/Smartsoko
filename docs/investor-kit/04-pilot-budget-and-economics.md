Fundraising target confirmed by founder: **TZS 5,000,000**. Everything below is a proposed planning model, not actual performance or a forecast.

This is an expense allocation; merchant settlement balances and payment float require separate cash planning. Track opening cash + investment + platform cash receipts − expenses − settlements = closing cash, with a separate liability reconciliation.

## Bottom-up opportunity model

Define one service area before estimating a market:

- Annual merchandise GMV = reachable households × purchasing share × orders per month × basket value × 12.
- Annual commission receipts = merchandise GMV × commission rate.
- Annual delivery receipts = fulfilled orders × delivery fee, treated consistently with contracts.

Use measured service-area household counts, interview findings and pilot conversion. Add a separate merchant capacity check: active merchants × sustainable orders per merchant per day × operating days. Reachable demand cannot be treated as fulfilled orders when supply or delivery capacity is lower. No numeric TAM is claimed in this draft.

## Proposed funding gates

1. **Before launch:** confirm service area, merchant agreements, rider quotes, payment configuration and test order-to-payout reconciliation. Resolve staging and deployment gaps.
2. **During pilot:** review spend, fulfilment, cancellations and contribution weekly. Release acquisition spending in small batches that operations can support.
3. **Before expansion:** assess repeat purchase cohorts, unsubsidised contribution, service reliability and reconciled settlements. Founder and investor must agree numeric thresholds and observation periods before launch.

## Weekly scorecard

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Active merchants | Merchants with at least one fulfilled paid order in the period |
| Completed paid orders | Fulfilled orders with confirmed payment; exclude tests and disclose COD treatment |
| GMV | Fulfilled merchandise value; report refunds and exclusions consistently |
| Net platform revenue | Earned platform fees after reversals; exclude merchant settlement liabilities |
| Contribution/order | Earned fees less all attributable variable costs, divided by fulfilled orders |
| Repeat purchase | Share of first-order customers with another completed order within a fixed window; use mature cohorts |
| Delivery reliability | Delivery-time distribution and share within the promised window |
| Cancellation rate | Cancelled valid orders divided by all valid placed orders |
| Acquisition cost | Attributable acquisition spend per new paying customer |
| Cash and settlements | Remaining operating cash, held customer money and unpaid merchant/driver liabilities |
