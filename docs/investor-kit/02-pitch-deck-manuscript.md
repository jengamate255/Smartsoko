# SmartSoko pitch deck manuscript

12-slide working draft · 19 October 2026. Copy below is intended for slides and presenter notes. Brackets require founder input. Proposed pilot strategy is not historical performance.

## 1. SmartSoko

**Local food and essentials, ordered from nearby merchants**

Seeking **TZS 5,000,000** for a focused pilot  
[Founder name] · [Contact]

Speaker notes: SmartSoko is a local marketplace connecting customers, merchants and delivery partners. We want to turn the product work into a measurable pilot in one service area.

## 2. The problem we will test

- Customers must find products, confirm availability and coordinate delivery.
- Merchants need a clear process for receiving and fulfilling online orders.
- Delivery needs enough nearby demand to work economically.

Speaker notes: These are customer and operating hypotheses. Add interview evidence and examples before presenting them as validated pain points. Avoid claiming all merchants or customers face the same problem.

## 3. One ordering workflow

Discover a merchant → select products → place an order → merchant fulfils → driver delivers

SmartSoko connects the customer storefront with merchant and driver workflows.

Speaker notes: Demonstrate a controlled order using test data. Payment options and delivery promises shown in a demo must match the validated pilot configuration. Show actual product screens rather than conceptual mockups presented as live features.

## 4. Product progress

- Web storefront and checkout implementation
- Merchant catalogue and order management
- Driver workflow and merchant finance implementation
- Mobile app projects alongside the web product

**Next gate: validate the full order, payment, delivery and payout lifecycle in staging**

Speaker notes: Repository evidence supports implementation progress. It does not establish active usage or readiness across every app. Recent merchant checks used isolated fixtures; stock protection notes describe a migration that was still undeployed at the time of that report. Merchant transfers are handled manually outside the platform and then recorded.

## 5. Payment access supports the opportunity

**75.78 million active mobile money subscriptions in Tanzania in 2025**

Mobile payments provide a relevant foundation for testing local commerce.

Subscriptions are not unique people, delivery buyers or our addressable market.

Speaker notes: Source: Bank of Tanzania, National Payment Systems Annual Report 2025, printed page 29: https://www.bot.go.tz/Publications/Regular/Annual%20Report/en/2026060211494565.pdf. The opportunity still depends on local demand, affordability and fulfilment reliability.

## 6. The first service area

**Proposed: one compact area in Dar es Salaam**

- Recruit a manageable group of food and essentials merchants
- Agree delivery hours, radius and pricing before opening orders
- Acquire initial customers through participating merchants
- Review paid demand and delivery costs each week

Speaker notes: Founder must confirm geography and category focus. Merchant recruitment counts should follow operational capacity. Avoid committing to citywide coverage before validating one area.

## 7. How SmartSoko could earn

Merchant commission + customer delivery fee

Less rider pay, payment costs, promotions, refunds and support costs

**The key measure: contribution per fulfilled order**

Speaker notes: This is a proposed commercial model. Actual revenue recognition depends on contracts and accounting treatment. GMV is merchandise sales value; merchant and driver balances are liabilities, not revenue. See the separate economics worksheet for an explicitly illustrative model.

## 9. Alternatives and our proposed focus

Customers can buy directly, call or message merchants, collect orders themselves, or use other delivery services.

Our proposed focus: accurate local catalogues, manageable merchant operations and dependable delivery within a small service area.

Speaker notes: These are positioning choices, not proven superiority. Add a dated comparison of actual alternatives in the selected area, including total price, merchant selection, service hours and observed fulfilment. Do not claim no competition or a technology moat.
