### The customer problem

Our hypothesis is that customers want a simpler way to find nearby food and essentials, place an order and coordinate delivery. Merchants need a manageable way to receive and fulfil those orders. Interviews and paid pilot orders will test which problems matter most and what customers will pay to solve them.

### The product

SmartSoko brings merchant discovery, product browsing, ordering and delivery coordination into a local marketplace. The repository contains a web storefront, customer checkout, merchant order management and driver workflows, alongside mobile app projects. Recent work includes merchant finance and payment reconciliation workflows. End-to-end staging and live operating validation remain necessary.

### The initial business

Start with a small group of food and essentials merchants in one compact delivery area. Help merchants publish accurate catalogues, acquire customers through those merchants and fulfil orders within a clearly stated service window. Expand only after repeat purchasing and delivery economics justify it.

The proposed revenue model is a merchant commission on fulfilled orders plus a customer delivery fee. Commission rates, merchant acceptance and delivery pricing are not yet confirmed. Merchandise sales value is GMV, not SmartSoko revenue.

### Why this market is worth testing

Tanzania has substantial mobile payment infrastructure: the Bank of Tanzania reports 75.78 million active mobile money subscriptions in 2025. These are subscriptions, not unique people or SmartSoko customers. This supports a payment-access thesis; it does not establish demand for delivery or market size. [Bank of Tanzania, National Payment Systems Annual Report 2025](https://www.bot.go.tz/Publications/Regular/Annual%20Report/en/2026060211494565.pdf), printed page 29.

### What the investment should establish

The proposed TZS 5 million budget supports merchant setup, delivery operations, customer acquisition, product reliability and a contingency reserve. Pilot reporting should show completed paid orders, repeat customers, cancellations, delivery time, customer acquisition cost and contribution after variable costs. The final pilot duration and targets will be set after merchant and delivery quotes are obtained.
