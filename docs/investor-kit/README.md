# SmartSoko investor kit

Working draft · 19 October 2026

Confirmed fundraising target: **TZS 5,000,000**. Currency and amount confirmed by the founder. Launch location, traction, team, valuation and investment terms remain unconfirmed.

Positioning: **SmartSoko connects local food and essentials merchants with customers and delivery partners.** Proposed fundraising story: fund a focused pilot and measure repeat purchasing and contribution per order.

## Materials

- [Investor one-pager](01-investor-one-pager.md): short introduction and funding proposal.
- [Pitch deck manuscript](02-pitch-deck-manuscript.md): 12 slides with speaking notes; editable content, not a rendered PowerPoint.
- [Pitch and outreach](03-pitch-and-outreach.md): spoken pitch, email and short message drafts. Nothing has been sent.
- [Pilot budget and economics](04-pilot-budget-and-economics.md): proposed allocation of the TZS 5 million and an illustrative per-order model.
- [Founder inputs and evidence](05-founder-inputs-and-evidence.md): missing facts, diligence questions and source register.

## How to use

Complete bracketed fields before external sharing. Proposed strategy and budget are recommendations for founder review, not existing commitments. Dar es Salaam is a provisional launch assumption based on product copy, not a confirmed operating location. No users, orders, revenue or merchant commitments have been verified for this kit; that does not mean they are zero.

The repository demonstrates product work, not commercial traction or production certification. This draft does not claim live payment reliability, automated payouts, regulatory approval, or a proven competitive advantage. Existing app and business files were left unchanged.
